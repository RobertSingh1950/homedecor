Buying guide

Home decor: the finishing touch of your home

Your home is your sanctuary. Even the most beautiful home benefits from a few finishing touches — here's how to choose home decor that is both functional and personal.

Knobs & handles

Door knobs and handles are often the first thing people touch when they enter your home. There is a wide range of styles to choose from, so you can find hardware that matches the overall look of your luxury home decor — or mix and match different styles to create a look that is entirely yours.

Hooks & hangers

Hooks and hangers add practical storage while doubling as a stylish focal point. Get creative with placement — hang hooks from a piece of driftwood or pair them with a metal sculpture for an arrangement guests will notice.

Showpieces & statement pieces

Showpieces add personality and interest to your home. Choose pieces that reflect your own story — a collection of antique clocks, a set of vintage teacups — and combine them to create a look no catalogue can reproduce.

Bronze oil lamps & chandeliers

Bronze oil lamps are a classic, elegant way to bring warmth to your home decor — place one prominently to make a statement. Chandeliers add formality and light; match the fixture's scale to the room before choosing a style.

Brass urlis & bronze idols

Brass urlis are traditional Indian vessels used for cooking, storing, and serving — and they make striking decorative bowls for flowers or floating candles. Bronze Ganesha figurines and other god idols are cherished home decor pieces, said to bring good luck, prosperity, and protection to the home.

Door stoppers & door knockers

Door stoppers keep doors from slamming while adding a note of character — try a lion's-paw stopper at your entrance. Door knockers are the traditional way to announce an arrival; size the knocker to the door and let it set the tone for the whole house.

Pichwai & Radha Krishna paintings

Pichwai paintings are a traditional Indian art form known for vibrant colors and intricate detail, bringing a piece of living culture to any space. Radha Krishna paintings depict the beloved Hindu deities and add beauty and romance to a room.
